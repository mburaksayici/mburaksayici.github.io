Room Redesign (“the app”) is made by Mehmet Burak Sayıcı. This page explains what the app sends, what it stores, and who receives it. It covers the iPhone app. It is not a privacy policy for the rest of this website.

Contact: [mburaksayici@gmail.com](mailto:mburaksayici@gmail.com), +90 531 919 54 55.

## Sending your data to AI providers (your permission)

Before the first design or search, the app asks for your permission to send data to two outside providers. You can choose “Not now”, and you can turn this off at any time in Me → AI data sharing. Without it, designing and searching are not available, and nothing is sent.

- **OpenAI** (an AI provider) receives your room photo, the product photos found for it, and the text you type, so it can understand the room and draw the redesigned image. OpenAI handles that data under its own API terms ([openai.com/policies](https://openai.com/policies)).
- **A web image search provider** receives the words you search for, so it can find furniture sold by other stores.

Both go through our server on Google Cloud first. We do not sell your data. The redesigned images are AI-generated.

## Room photos

We do not store your room photos or the redesigned images on our server. They pass through it to the AI provider and back to your phone. Your rooms and their images are saved on your phone, and they are removed from it when you delete them or delete the app.

## Text, searches, and likes

The text you type, the searches you run, the products you like or dismiss, and the assistant’s replies are stored on our server so a conversation can continue and appear in Discoveries. They are kept until you delete your account.

## Product search

We show products found on other stores’ websites and link you to the merchant. We do not sell the furniture and we do not receive your purchases from those stores. Search results for the same words and country may be kept for up to 60 days to avoid repeating a search.

## Account and purchases

You sign in with Sign in with Apple. It is required, because your credits belong to your Apple account. We store only Apple’s user id for you. The app does not ask Apple for your name or email. If you sign in on a new phone with the same Apple ID, your credits come back.

Purchases are processed by Apple. We store the product you bought and the transaction id so credits can be granted once and a refund can be applied. We do not receive your card number. Apple keeps its own record of the purchase.

The app also keeps a random install id on your phone to tell this installation apart.

## Country, language, and service protection

We store the country and language you choose so searches use the right stores and language. To protect the service from abuse, the app proves it is a genuine copy using Apple’s App Attest, and we count requests per account. We also record basic usage events (such as which screen you opened or that you opened a store page) linked to your account to understand which features are used.

## How long we keep it

We keep the account, conversation text, usage events, and purchase records until you delete the account in the app (Me → Delete my account). Deleting removes that user, their conversations, and unused credits from our server. It cannot be undone. Apple may still keep the purchase in your Apple ID. Signing out only disconnects this phone; your credits stay with your Apple ID.

## Tracking

We do not use advertising identifiers. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not sell personal information.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.

## Your choices

You can withdraw AI data sharing in the app, delete your account in the app, or email us to ask what we hold about you.
